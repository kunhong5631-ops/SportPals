SportPals is for organizing real games with real people. You agree **not** to post

- harasses, threatens, bullies or discriminates against anyone;
- is sexually explicit, violent, hateful or otherwise objectionable;

- impersonates someone else or is deliberately misleading;
- breaks the law or encourages others to.

**There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.**

- You can report a message, player, game or tournament from inside the app.

Use good judgment when meeting people, follow the rules of the venue, and play
within your abilities. SportPals isn't responsible for injuries, losses or
disputes arising from games or tournaments.
