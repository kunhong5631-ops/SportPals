# Delete your SportPals account

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open SportPals and tap your profile picture (top right of the map).
2. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Without the app

Email **kunhong5631@gmail.com** from the email address you signed up with, with
the subject **"Delete my SportPals account"**. We will confirm the request and
delete your account and data within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your sign-in account and profile (name, bio, favorite sports)
- Your profile photo
- Every message you sent in game group chats
- Games and tournaments you host
- Your spot in games and tournaments you joined

Nothing is kept afterwards, except operational logs held by our service
providers, which they delete on their standard schedules.
