# SportPals Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026

SportPals ("the app", "we") helps people find, host and join pickup games and
tournaments nearby. This policy explains what information the app collects, how
it is used, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## Information we collect

**Account information**

- Your email address and password, used to sign in. Passwords are handled by
  Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
- The display name you choose.

**Profile information (optional)**

- A short bio and your favorite sports.
- A profile photo you upload, or a preset avatar you pick.

**Content you create**

- Games and tournaments you host: sport, title, notes, tags, date, time and the
  location you choose for them.
- Which games and tournaments you join, and teams you register.
- Messages you send in a game's group chat.

**Location**

- If you allow it, the app uses your device's location **on your device** to
  center the map, show how far away games are, and suggest nearby spots.
- We do not store your device location on our servers, and other users never
  see it.
- Locations you choose for games and tournaments are stored and shown to other
  users, because that's how people find them.

**Feedback you send**
- The feedback message, its type (bug, idea or other), your display name, device
  type and app version. Your email address is included only if you choose to let
  us reply. Feedback is emailed to us and deleted with your account.

**Reports and blocks**
- If you report content or a player, we store the report (including a copy of a
  reported message) so we can review it. Reports are visible only to us.
- Your list of blocked players is private to you; blocked players aren't told.

**Technical information**

- Our service providers (listed below) may log technical data such as IP
  address, device type and error reports in order to operate and secure the
  service.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
- To show games and tournaments on the map and let you host and join them.
- To show who is playing, run tournament brackets, and deliver group chat messages.
- To look up a spot's posted opening hours when you ask for them.
- To keep the service working, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do **not** sell your information, show ads, or use your information for
advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

## What other users can see

Other signed-in SportPals users can see:

- Your display name, profile photo or avatar, bio and favorite sports.
- Games and tournaments you host or join, including your name on their rosters
  and tournament teams.
- Messages you send in the group chat of a game, visible to that game's players.

Other users **cannot** see your email address or your device location.

## Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf, under
their own privacy terms:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud
  Functions): stores your account, profile, photo, games, tournaments and chat
  messages. Data is stored in the United States.
- **Mapbox**: displays the map and powers place search. Mapbox receives the map
  area being viewed, search text you type, and your IP address. Mapbox usage
  telemetry is turned off in the app.
- **OpenStreetMap (Overpass API)**: when you tap "See opening hours", our
  server sends the spot's coordinates to an OpenStreetMap server to look up
  posted hours. No information about you is included in that request.

## How long we keep information

- **Your account and profile** are kept until you delete your account.
- **Games and their group chats** are deleted automatically 30 days after the
  game ends.
- **Tournaments** are deleted automatically about 30 days after they take place.
- Your block list is deleted with your account. Reports you filed are kept for
  safety review, since they may concern another user's behavior.
- Operational logs kept by our service providers are deleted on their standard
  schedules.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

- **In the app:** Profile → **Delete account**.
- **Without the app:** see [Delete your SportPals account](delete-account.md).

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, profile photo, sign-in
account, every chat message you sent, and the games and tournaments you host.
You are also removed from games and tournaments you joined.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to stored data is
restricted by security rules so that users can only change their own
information.

## Children

SportPals is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for
significant changes, let you know in the app.
